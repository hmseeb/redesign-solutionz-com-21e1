How it works

The security check, in plain words.

Three things happen between your click and the site. All of them are automatic, and all of them finish in about a second.

Step 1

Checking the site connection security

Your request is inspected before any page content is sent. Real browsers pass quietly; automated traffic, credential-stuffing tools and scrapers get stopped here instead of deeper in the site.

Step 2

Loading from the nearest edge

Once you clear the check, the page is delivered from a content delivery network — a cache near you rather than one distant server. That is the animated loader you see for a moment.

Step 3

Cookies confirm it was you

A small cookie records that your browser already passed, so you are not re-checked on every click. Block it and the check restarts forever — which is exactly why the cookie notice appears.

Short version: enable cookies, reload once, and solutionz.com behaves normally from then on.

Fix it yourself

“This page requires cookies to be enabled.”

That message means your browser settings are blocking the check, not that the site is down. Pick your browser and follow the steps — it takes under a minute.

Chrome — desktop & Android
  1. Open Settings → Privacy and security.
  2. Choose Third-party cookies (older versions: Cookies and other site data).
  3. Select Allow third-party cookies, or add solutionz.com under Sites allowed to use cookies.
  4. Return here and reload the page.
Safari — macOS & iOS
  1. macOS: Safari → Settings → Privacy, then untick Block all cookies.
  2. iPhone / iPad: Settings app → Apps → Safari → turn Block All Cookies off.
  3. Also switch off Prevent Cross-Site Tracking if the check still loops.
  4. Reload solutionz.com.
Firefox
  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Set Enhanced Tracking Protection to Standard.
  3. Or click the shield icon in the address bar and turn protection off for this site.
  4. Reload the page.
Edge
  1. Open Settings → Cookies and site permissions.
  2. Choose Manage and delete cookies and site data.
  3. Turn on Allow sites to save and read cookie data.
  4. Reload the page.
Still stuck after all that?
  1. Try a private/incognito window with cookies allowed.
  2. Pause any ad-blocking or privacy extension for this site.
  3. Switch off a VPN or corporate proxy, which can trip the check.
  4. If it still will not clear, tell us what you see and we will unblock you.
